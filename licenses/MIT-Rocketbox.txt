Oamenii din scena 3D (src/scene/people.dat si src/scene/people.webp, facute de scripts/people.mjs) vin din
biblioteca Microsoft Rocketbox, https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox: personajele Female_Party_01,
Female_Party_02, Female_Adult_03, Female_Adult_08, Female_Adult_12, Female_Adult_17, Male_Adult_01, Male_Adult_06,
Male_Adult_09, Male_Adult_16, Male_Adult_17 si Male_Adult_18, puse in poze cu miscarile f/m_idle_neutral_01 si
f/m_walk_neutral_01 din aceeasi biblioteca, simplificate, cu texturile micsorate intr-un atlas.
